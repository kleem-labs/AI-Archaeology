# Excavation 000 — Before Mathematics Existed

## The Problem

Imagine waking up in a world with no mathematics, no language, no physics, and no books.

What do you actually possess?

Only observations.

You see trees, rivers, animals, shadows, movement, and sound.

Everything that humanity will ever know begins as observations.

---

## First Discovery

You see one tiger today.

You see another tiger tomorrow.

How do you know they are both tigers?

You keep only the important properties:

- Four legs
- Orange
- Stripes
- Tail
- Sharp teeth

Without realizing it, you have invented **features**.

---

## Key Insight

Intelligence is impossible without compression.

You cannot remember every atom of every tiger.

You must keep only what matters.

---

## Challenge

Suppose tomorrow you find a tiger with three legs.

Is it still a tiger?

Which features are essential, and which are not?

This question leads naturally to the next excavation:
**Why Features Exist.**
