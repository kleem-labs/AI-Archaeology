## Cinematic continuity sequence

Create one continuous five-frame scene—not five unrelated illustrations:

1. **Question:** How can one observation survive after every witness has gone?
2. **Object:** a fresh tiger track beside the tribe's first charcoal mark
3. **Failure:** Wind softens the track while two witnesses remember different animals.
4. **Transformation:** A hand traces the track into clay; the animal can leave, but the shared mark remains.
5. **Seal:** Mathematics begins when an observation receives a form that can outlive its observer.

Keep the same object, lighting, camera direction, and landmarks in every frame. The final frame may reveal notation faintly, but no equation may appear before the physical transformation has made every operation necessary.
