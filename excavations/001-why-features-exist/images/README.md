## Cinematic continuity sequence

Create one continuous five-frame scene—not five unrelated illustrations:

1. **Question:** Which parts of an animal must we preserve to judge whether it threatens the camp?
2. **Object:** five carved tiger tokens: weight, speed, teeth, stripes, and direction
3. **Failure:** A zebra passes the stripe test, while a three-legged tiger fails the four-leg test.
4. **Transformation:** The tokens separate, and only observations relevant to the danger decision remain on the table.
5. **Seal:** A feature is a chosen observation that preserves a distinction needed by a question.

Keep the same object, lighting, camera direction, and landmarks in every frame. The final frame may reveal notation faintly, but no equation may appear before the physical transformation has made every operation necessary.
