# Exercises — Vectors as Change

## Calculate

1. Find the displacement from `[2, 3]` to `[7, 1]` and verify it by addition.
2. Compose changes `[1, 2]`, `[-3, 4]`, and `[2, -1]`.
3. Scale `[6, -2]` by `0.5`, `-1`, and `0`. Interpret each result.

## Reason

4. Explain why distance cannot reconstruct a destination but displacement can.
5. Distinguish a position, a velocity, and an acceleration even though all may be arrays.
6. Give a nonphysical example where vector difference represents meaningful change.

## Experiment

7. Run `implementation.py` and move the waypoint while preserving start and finish.
8. Add a third leg to the journey and verify that composition is associative.
9. Write a function that interpolates halfway between two points using subtraction, scaling, and addition.

## Reconstruct

10. Represent a student's skill profile before and after a course. Interpret the change vector carefully, including what it cannot prove.
