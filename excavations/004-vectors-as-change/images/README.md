## Cinematic continuity sequence

Create one continuous five-frame scene—not five unrelated illustrations:

1. **Question:** How can a vector describe not only what exists, but what changed?
2. **Object:** an arrow laid from yesterday's tiger position to today's
3. **Failure:** Two isolated position stones reveal locations but hide the movement connecting them.
4. **Transformation:** The stones remain while a directed arrow grows from the earlier position to the later one.
5. **Seal:** A change vector remembers both how far the world moved and in which direction.

Keep the same object, lighting, camera direction, and landmarks in every frame. The final frame may reveal notation faintly, but no equation may appear before the physical transformation has made every operation necessary.
