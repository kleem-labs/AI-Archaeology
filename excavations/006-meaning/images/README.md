## Cinematic continuity sequence

Create one continuous five-frame scene—not five unrelated illustrations:

1. **Question:** If a dictionary cannot contain every use of a word, where does meaning come from?
2. **Object:** the word tiger suspended in a web of neighboring word-threads
3. **Failure:** Cutting tiger from its sentences leaves a label whose living uses have disappeared.
4. **Transformation:** Threads reconnect it to hunt, stripes, jungle, fear, and many contrasting contexts.
5. **Seal:** Meaning is not hidden inside a word; it is constrained by the relationships in which the word participates.

Keep the same object, lighting, camera direction, and landmarks in every frame. The final frame may reveal notation faintly, but no equation may appear before the physical transformation has made every operation necessary.
