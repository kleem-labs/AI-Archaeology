## Cinematic continuity sequence

Create one continuous five-frame scene—not five unrelated illustrations:

1. **Question:** What if understanding one word requires several kinds of relevance at once?
2. **Object:** a many-windowed observatory aimed at the same sentence
3. **Failure:** One attention beam must choose between syntax, identity, position, and reference, flattening different relationships into one compromise.
4. **Transformation:** Several windows open; each follows one kind of relationship before their views reunite.
5. **Seal:** Multi-head attention lets several relational questions be asked in parallel before their answers meet.

Keep the same object, lighting, camera direction, and landmarks in every frame. The final frame may reveal notation faintly, but no equation may appear before the physical transformation has made every operation necessary.
