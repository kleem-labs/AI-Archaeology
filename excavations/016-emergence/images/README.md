## Cinematic continuity sequence

Create one continuous five-frame scene—not five unrelated illustrations:

1. **Question:** How can countless small learned relationships become abilities nobody wrote as rules?
2. **Object:** a dark word-constellation whose hidden figure appears only when all stars glow
3. **Failure:** Inspecting one star or one rule reveals almost nothing resembling the complete ability.
4. **Transformation:** Connections brighten across many layers until a larger structure becomes visible between them.
5. **Seal:** Emergence is the visible capability formed by many distributed relationships acting together.

Keep the same object, lighting, camera direction, and landmarks in every frame. The final frame may reveal notation faintly, but no equation may appear before the physical transformation has made every operation necessary.
