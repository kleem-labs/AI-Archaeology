## Cinematic continuity sequence

Create one continuous five-frame scene—not five unrelated illustrations:

1. **Question:** Where Should Learning Begin?
2. **Object:** the initialization scale mounted on the ring of glass lanterns
3. **Failure:** The scale follows the tempting path—set every weight to zero. Then the evidence answers: neurons receive identical evidence and remain identical. Use arbitrarily huge random values. Signals explode or gates saturate.
4. **Transformation:** The keeper of uncertain stories changes one moving part. The scale can now draw small random weights whose scale depends on how many inputs feed the neuron.
5. **Seal:** Initialization keeps the missing power: draw small random weights whose scale depends on how many inputs feed the neuron.

Keep the same object, lighting, camera direction, and landmarks in every frame. The final frame may reveal notation faintly, but no equation may appear before the physical transformation has made every operation necessary.
