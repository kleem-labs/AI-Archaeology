# Mistakes Worth Preserving — Excavation 202

## The tempting idea

We tried to place connected objects in the same set and assume co-membership tells us the nature and direction of their connection.

## The evidence that refused it

putting tiger, river, and village into one collection cannot distinguish tiger-near-river from village-reports-tiger. It also cannot distinguish an arrow from tiger to river from the reverse arrow.

## What the wreckage taught us

The next construction had to store each connection as an ordered pair and let a named relation be the set of all pairs carrying the same kind of edge.

Keep this wrong idea. It is the negative space around Relations: it records why the accepted method has exactly the responsibilities it does.
