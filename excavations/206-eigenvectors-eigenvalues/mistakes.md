# Mistakes Worth Preserving — Excavation 206

## The tempting idea

We tried to track every coordinate of every repeatedly transformed arrow and hope the long-term pattern becomes obvious.

## The evidence that refused it

coordinate expressions grow while the persistent behavior stays hidden. Two initial arrows can look unrelated even when repeated transformation eventually makes both align with the same dominant direction.

## What the wreckage taught us

The next construction had to search for nonzero directions that the transformation only scales, and record the corresponding scale factors.

Keep this wrong idea. It is the negative space around Eigenvectors and Eigenvalues: it records why the accepted method has exactly the responsibilities it does.
