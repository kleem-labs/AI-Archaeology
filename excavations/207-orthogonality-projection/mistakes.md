# Mistakes Worth Preserving — Excavation 207

## The tempting idea

We tried to copy whichever coordinate looks largest or slide to an arbitrary point on the allowed rail.

## The evidence that refused it

the chosen point changes when coordinates are renamed and gives no proof that another allowed point is not closer. The discarded error may still point partly along the rail, revealing that more of the track could have been retained.

## What the wreckage taught us

The next construction had to choose the shadow whose leftover error is perpendicular to the allowed direction, because then no further movement along the rail can reduce the distance.

Keep this wrong idea. It is the negative space around Orthogonality and Projection: it records why the accepted method has exactly the responsibilities it does.
