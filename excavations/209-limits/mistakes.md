# Mistakes Worth Preserving — Excavation 209

## The tempting idea

We tried to declare that a sequence reaches its destination only when one finite term equals the destination exactly.

## The evidence that refused it

the gaps `1/2, 1/4, 1/8, ...` never equal zero, so the rule denies the visible fact that they can be made smaller than any requested tolerance.

## What the wreckage taught us

The next construction had to define the destination by a guarantee: however tiny a permitted error is chosen, all sufficiently late terms fall inside it.

Keep this wrong idea. It is the negative space around Limits: it records why the accepted method has exactly the responsibilities it does.
