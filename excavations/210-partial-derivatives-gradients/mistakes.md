# Mistakes Worth Preserving — Excavation 210

## The tempting idea

We tried to compute one ordinary derivative as if the entire parameter vector were a single undifferentiated number.

## The evidence that refused it

the answer cannot say which dial caused which part of the change or which physical direction rises fastest. Different paths through the same point produce different slopes.

## What the wreckage taught us

The next construction had to hold every other dial fixed to measure one partial derivative at a time, then gather those coordinate sensitivities into the gradient vector.

Keep this wrong idea. It is the negative space around Partial Derivatives and Gradients: it records why the accepted method has exactly the responsibilities it does.
