# Mistakes Worth Preserving — Excavation 212

## The tempting idea

We tried to declare every zero-gradient point a successful minimum and stop moving.

## The evidence that refused it

the saddle also has zero first-order slope. Stopping there mistakes balanced opposing curvature for completion, while choosing a large step without curvature can leap across a narrow bowl.

## What the wreckage taught us

The next construction had to differentiate the gradient again and store how every pair of coordinates changes the local slope.

Keep this wrong idea. It is the negative space around Hessians and Curvature: it records why the accepted method has exactly the responsibilities it does.
