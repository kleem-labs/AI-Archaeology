# Mistakes Worth Preserving — Excavation 213

## The tempting idea

We tried to extend the tangent line indefinitely and assume constant slope everywhere.

## The evidence that refused it

for a curved signal the linear prediction drifts, and doubling h can more than double the error. The tangent remembers direction but forgets that the direction itself changes.

## What the wreckage taught us

The next construction had to build a local polynomial: start with the known value, add slope times displacement, then add curvature times squared displacement with the counting factor required by repeated differentiation.

Keep this wrong idea. It is the negative space around Taylor Approximation: it records why the accepted method has exactly the responsibilities it does.
