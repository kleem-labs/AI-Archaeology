# Visual Brief — Integrals — Reconstructing a Whole from Infinitesimal Pieces

Create a five-frame mathematical fantasy sequence inside **The River of Change**.

1. **Question:** How can a changing rate become the total water actually delivered?
2. **Object:** show a river gauge and thousands of increasingly thin glass cups.
3. **Failure:** One noon reading is multiplied across the whole day, granting dawn and dusk a rate they never had.
4. **Transformation:** Let each tiny interval fill its own cup at its own rate, add the cups, and make them thinner until coarse partition error disappears.
5. **Memory seal:** reveal “An integral rebuilds a whole by accumulating locally honest pieces.”

Preserve the same named objects, camera direction, and visual landmarks across
all five frames so the viewer experiences one changing world rather than five
illustrations. The formula may appear only as a faint final engraving after the
transformation. The image succeeds when a reader can cover the caption and
reconstruct the mathematical promise from the scene alone.
