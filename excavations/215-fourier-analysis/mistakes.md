# Mistakes Worth Preserving — Excavation 215

## The tempting idea

We tried to compare waveforms only sample by sample in time.

## The evidence that refused it

the same note shifted slightly appears very different at every position, and two overlapping tones hide inside one jagged trace. Time coordinates expose when, not which frequency.

## What the wreckage taught us

The next construction had to compare the signal with a family of rotating sine-and-cosine patterns and add the agreements, producing one coefficient for each candidate frequency.

Keep this wrong idea. It is the negative space around Fourier Analysis: it records why the accepted method has exactly the responsibilities it does.
