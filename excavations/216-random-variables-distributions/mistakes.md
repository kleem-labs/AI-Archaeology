# Mistakes Worth Preserving — Excavation 216

## The tempting idea

We tried to treat the outcome label itself as a number and perform arithmetic directly on names such as ‘no sighting’ and ‘two sightings’.

## The evidence that refused it

outcomes may be stories, images, or paths rather than numbers, and the same numerical question can group many different outcomes. Arithmetic needs a mapping from possible worlds to values.

## What the wreckage taught us

The next construction had to define a random variable as a function assigning a numerical value to every outcome, then transfer probability mass through that mapping to form its distribution.

Keep this wrong idea. It is the negative space around Random Variables and Distributions: it records why the accepted method has exactly the responsibilities it does.
