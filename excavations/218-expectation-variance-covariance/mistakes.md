# Mistakes Worth Preserving — Excavation 218

## The tempting idea

We tried to report only the average and treat distributions sharing it as interchangeable.

## The evidence that refused it

the average hides spread. It also cannot reveal whether tiger count and alarm count rise together or move independently, which matters when one is used to predict the other.

## What the wreckage taught us

The next construction had to compute expectation as a probability-weighted centre, variance as average squared departure from that centre, and covariance as average product of paired departures.

Keep this wrong idea. It is the negative space around Expectation, Variance, and Covariance: it records why the accepted method has exactly the responsibilities it does.
