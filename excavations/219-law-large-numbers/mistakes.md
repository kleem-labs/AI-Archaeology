# Mistakes Worth Preserving — Excavation 219

## The tempting idea

We tried to demand that every short sample reproduce the population expectation exactly.

## The evidence that refused it

chance has not failed when the first three tosses are all heads. Short runs fluctuate, so exact equality would reject honest randomness and make estimation impossible.

## What the wreckage taught us

The next construction had to study the sample mean as the number of independent observations grows and ask whether the probability of a substantial error shrinks toward zero.

Keep this wrong idea. It is the negative space around The Law of Large Numbers: it records why the accepted method has exactly the responsibilities it does.
