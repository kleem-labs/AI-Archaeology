# Mistakes Worth Preserving — Excavation 220

## The tempting idea

We tried to assume the average has the same distributional shape as each individual disturbance.

## The evidence that refused it

averaging changes scale and shape. A single skewed measurement and the mean of one hundred such measurements do not have the same uncertainty.

## What the wreckage taught us

The next construction had to centre the sample mean at μ, divide by its standard error σ/√n, and study the distribution of that normalized error as n grows.

Keep this wrong idea. It is the negative space around The Central Limit Theorem: it records why the accepted method has exactly the responsibilities it does.
