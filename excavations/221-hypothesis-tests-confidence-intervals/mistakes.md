# Mistakes Worth Preserving — Excavation 221

## The tempting idea

We tried to declare every positive sample difference a discovery.

## The evidence that refused it

another sample from unchanged systems can land above zero by chance. A positive sign says which side won this sample; it does not say how surprising that victory would be if the true average difference were zero.

## What the wreckage taught us

The next construction had to state the no-improvement claim, measure the observed mean difference in units of its standard error, and report both a test statistic and the range of effects compatible with the sampling noise.

Keep this wrong idea. It is the negative space around Hypothesis Tests and Confidence Intervals: it records why the accepted method has exactly the responsibilities it does.
