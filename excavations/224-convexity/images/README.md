# Visual Brief — Convexity — A Landscape Without Hidden Valleys

Create a five-frame mathematical fantasy sequence inside **The Garden of Futures**.

1. **Question:** When can a nearby valley be trusted as the lowest valley anywhere?
2. **Object:** show a taut golden string stretched above a single clay bowl.
3. **Failure:** On a rippled floor, the traveler settles in a shallow pocket while a deeper valley hides beyond a ridge.
4. **Transformation:** Stretch the string between any two points. If the landscape always remains below its chord, no hidden ridge can protect a better local valley.
5. **Memory seal:** reveal “Convexity is the promise that a landscape contains no secret lower valley.”

Preserve the same named objects, camera direction, and visual landmarks across
all five frames so the viewer experiences one changing world rather than five
illustrations. The formula may appear only as a faint final engraving after the
transformation. The image succeeds when a reader can cover the caption and
reconstruct the mathematical promise from the scene alone.
