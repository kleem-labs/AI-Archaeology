# Mistakes Worth Preserving — Excavation 224

## The tempting idea

We tried to trust any small local minimum as the best possible solution.

## The evidence that refused it

on a rippled landscape, a nearby valley may be higher than another valley beyond a ridge. Local slope alone cannot certify that no better point exists elsewhere.

## What the wreckage taught us

The next construction had to require every chord between two points to lie on or above the function, preventing a hidden hump from separating a local minimum from a lower global one.

Keep this wrong idea. It is the negative space around Convexity: it records why the accepted method has exactly the responsibilities it does.
